# Add bep20 VC to Trust Wallet

Adding BEP20 VinuChain Token ($VC) on Trust Wallet - Beginners

## Introduction

VinuChain is a cutting-edge blockchain ecosystem built on a DAG ledger structure, providing fast transactions, scalability, and most importantly, a determinably feeless experience. It rewards stakers for liquidity provision and supports smart contracts and decentralized applications.



## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

•       A smartphone or tablet with Trust Wallet installed.

•       A secure and reliable internet connection.

•       The BNB Smart Chain-BSC (BEP20) contract address.

Always confirm this address on [BscScan](https://bscscan.com/token/0x2bf83d080d8bc4715984e75e5b3d149805d11751)[.](https://bscscan.com/token/0x2bf83d080d8bc4715984e75e5b3d149805d11751)&#x20;

## 1.  Install Trust Wallet

If you don't already have Trust Wallet on your device, follow these steps to download and set it up. If you already have the app, you can skip to the next step.

•       Visit your device's app store (App Store for iOS or Google Play Store for Android).

•       Search for "Trust Wallet" and click on the official Trust Wallet app.

•       Download and install the app on your device.

•       Open Trust Wallet and agree to the terms and conditions.

&#x20;

## 2. Create or Import a Wallet

If you don't already have a Trust Wallet, you'll need to create one. If you already have one, you can skip to the next step.

To create a wallet:

•       Click "Create a new wallet."

•       Set a strong password and back it up securely.

•       Follow the on-screen instructions to create your wallet.

If you already have a Trust Wallet:

•       Open the app and enter your PIN or use your preferred security method.

&#x20;

## 3. Add $VC (BEP20) to Trust Wallet

Now, let's add the $VC token to your wallet:

•       Open Trust Wallet and click on the “Tokens” tab.

•       Scroll to the bottom of the page.

•       Select “Add Tokens” as in the first image below.

•       Then select “+” button.&#x20;

•       Click on the “Network” to change the selection from “Ethereum” to “BNB Smart Chain”.

•       Select “BNB Smart Chain”

•       Enter the VinuChain contract address in the “Contract Address” field.&#x20;

•       Click “Save”

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png>)&#x20;



If the VinuChain Name, Symbol and Decimals doesn't appear automatically:

Enter the following details:&#x20;

* Name: VinuChain
* Symbol: VC
* Decimals: 18

Click 'Save.'

&#x20;

## 4. View Your $VC Balance

After successfully adding $VC to your wallet, you can now view your $VC balance:

•       Go back to the 'Wallets' tab.

•       Scroll down until you find your $VC balance.

&#x20;

## 5. Send or Receive $VC

To send or receive $VC:

•       Click on your $VC balance.

•       Choose “Send” to send $VC to another wallet or “Receive” to receive $VC.

•       Follow the on-screen instructions.

&#x20;

## **Conclusion**

Congratulations! You have successfully added VinuChain Token ($VC) to your Trust Wallet. You can now manage, send, and receive $VC with ease. Remember to keep your wallet secure and back up your important information.
